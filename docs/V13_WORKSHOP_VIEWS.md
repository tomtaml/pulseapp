# V1.3 workshop views

The facilitator can open `https://pulse-srf-variant-preview.tom-tamlander.workers.dev/v13-setup.html`, choose a site and a starting view, then switch each question module on or off. The page creates a shareable preview link. No response is submitted or stored by these workshop views; record any approved observations using the workshop's separate procedure.

| Starting view | Scenario | Task and understanding questions | SUS | Service confidence, trust and outcome scales |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demo only (`view=demo`) | Yes | No | No | No |
| Demo plus questions (`view=questions`) | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Full preview (`view=full`) | Yes | Yes | Direct users only | Yes |

The facilitator can alter any of the last three columns with `questions=0|1`, `sus=0|1` and `scales=0|1`. The old `demo=1` link still forces demo only. A link with `view=` or an individual module switch always stays in preview mode, even if opened on an environment where the complete research instrument is available. Research submissions still require the complete approved instrument and server-side collection gates; these switches never relax the server validation.

The scenario pages appear in all views. The Finnish fleet route `/v13-fleet.html` runs the full RC1 mobile interaction stack: alignment guidance, next-delivery constraints, V2G permission and offer, animated charging and export cycle, override and winter recovery. Its questions, SUS and trust screens are enabled by the same facilitator switches. Links to `/v13.html?variant=fi-fleet` redirect to that route.

The Trikala route now follows one passenger-car stop at an illustrative wireless bay. The participant checks alignment, chooses a 55–75% next-trip minimum and a 21:15 or 22:30 departure, then compares charging now with a later lower-price or higher renewable-availability window. The app marks a window unavailable when its estimated ready time plus a 15-minute margin exceeds departure. The 60 kWh battery, 45% arrival, 75% target, effective 9 kW charging, 18 kWh energy, €0.20–0.30/kWh charging examples, €0.20/kWh gross export payment and renewable indicator are all fictional workshop assumptions, not site tariffs, forecasts or provenance. Price and renewable availability are separate signals.

A hot-weather start-delay stress test at the same bay appears before the animated session. A retry shifts the estimate by 30 minutes; an infeasible retry prompts an earlier option or support. Charge now and assisted charge-now choices use the earlier window; no support contact is sent. The participant can run, pause, step or stop the charging example, which displays energy grid → car, the protected minimum and separate charging cost. An optional V2G offer appears only once charging has completed above the chosen reserve. Export remains off by default, may be declined or stopped, and cannot exceed the reserve. Gross export payment is distinct from charging cost and hypothetical replacement energy; battery wear, losses and fees are unknown. The broader [single-stop participant journey](V13_TRIKALA_CONCEPT_DRAFT.md) still lists site decisions to validate.

With `questions=1`, six optional Trikala task probes and four case-specific understanding questions test positioning, price versus renewable cues, timing, permission, value and recovery. Wording and Greek translation require site and ethics review; these preview responses are unscored and never submitted. SUS and shared scales remain optional through the workshop setup page. The Trikala journey stays preview-only even in a collection-enabled environment until its revised instrument and research contract are approved.

The UK route now follows one illustrative home V2H journey. After the simulation acknowledgement, the participant checks the surroundings and can begin a guided manoeuvre beside the house. A staged obstacle stops parking automatically; the participant can also stop during movement. Once the obstacle and entrance path have been reviewed, they can resume guided parking or use the Tampere-style arrow pad: forward above, left/right around a central vehicle marker, and back below, with one highlighted instruction at a time and feedback for wrong directions. A separate simulated guidance fault requires another check before manual movement. Stop remains available throughout. The route cannot continue until the vehicle is parked and the access route confirmed clear. These controls simulate a proposed interface, not tested vehicle sensing or autonomous parking. The separate street bay, rain/gully fallback and regular-charge choice have been removed from this Oxfordshire route.

The overnight V2H example follows directly. For the demonstration, limited home-support permission is an explicit story assumption and can be cancelled or stopped at any time. Before running, the participant selects a minimum morning car charge of 65%, 70%, 75% or 80%. The participant can run, pause, step, skip or replay eight checkpoints from 18:00 to 07:00: the car begins at 50%, charges to 80%, then supplies some home demand while protecting the chosen morning minimum. At the default 70% minimum, the ledger shows 18.0 kWh stored in the car, 6.0 kWh taken from it for V2H, 5.4 kWh delivered to the house, and a £0.72 illustrative energy cost difference. The arithmetic assumes 60 kWh usable battery capacity, 90% delivery, 30p/kWh household import and 15p/kWh replacement energy. Recharge losses, battery wear and fees are excluded. These are not measured results or live tariffs; stopping before export shows zero V2H energy and zero cost difference. The physical home connection, household essential-load rule and accessible support process still require site review.

The UK route remains storage-free even if a non-preview link is opened on a collection-enabled environment: its former scenario/recovery choice fields are no longer elicited by this focused journey, so the existing V1.3 submission contract must be revised and approved before UK collection. With questions enabled, a draft Oxfordshire task page probes positioning, recovery, reserve, energy direction, household value, override and support responsibility; manual-arrow clarity appears only if used. Four UK-specific understanding questions follow. These draft items are optional and unscored; see [Oxfordshire instrument draft](V13_OXFORDSHIRE_INSTRUMENT_DRAFT.md). The optional shared scales cover V2H intention, accessible controls and operator trust without asking street-WPT intention for an unseen street scene. Workshop observation can use a separate approved facilitator record. The display retains large text, high contrast, participant-triggered read-aloud and reduced-motion styling. The Finnish flow and partner V1.2 RC1 Worker are unchanged.

SUS is presented only to roles marked as direct users: the fleet driver, Greek passenger driver and UK accessible driver. The dispatcher and fleet manager can see comprehension and confidence/trust items but not SUS. The Finnish fleet route uses the RC1 Finnish working translation for comprehension, SUS and trust when `lang=fi`; those are for cognitive testing and must not be treated as approved scores or the final V1.3 research instrument. Greek study questions and scales remain in English pending approval. UK assistive-technology and participant review is still needed before field use.
